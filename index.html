Our Church & Leadership

Our History

End Time Chapel is a vibrant branch of the Assemblies of God, Ghana, beautifully located in Berekum, Domfete.

The assembly was gracefully founded in the year 2015 by the visionary pioneer, Rev. Noah Yaw Kumah, setting a solid foundation of faith, gospel truth, and community transformation.

Current Lead Pastor

Serving passionately as the son in the Lord to our founder, carrying forward the mantle of ministry with dedication.
