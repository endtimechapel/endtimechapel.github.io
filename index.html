Our History & Leadership

End Time Chapel is a vibrant branch of the Assemblies of God, Ghana. Located at Berekum, Domfete, our church was founded in the year 2015 by the visionary spiritual leader, Rev. Noah Yaw Kumah. Over the years, the chapel has stood as a beacon of hope, truth, and transforming grace within the community.

Rev. Afari Isaac

Current Lead Pastor (Son in the Lord to the Founder)

Rev. Afari Isaac leads the congregation with deep biblical insight and devotion. He is happily married to his partner in ministry, Mrs. Hellen Mensah. They are beautifully blessed with two wonderful children, Phebe and Kingsley.
